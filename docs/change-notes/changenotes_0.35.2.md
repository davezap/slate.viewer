# Slate Viewer 0.35.2

- **My Submissions from the account menu.** On metatrash.com, the account menu (top right) opens My Submissions, so you don't need to be on a form.
- **A count of what's waiting for you.** The account icon shows a red number: files to read, forms to review and forms sent back to you. The menu says which.
- **Green ticks for approved forms**, in My Submissions and next to the filed copies in the file tree. Declined forms have no tick; forms sent back to you are marked in red.
- **Requests for more stand out.** When a reviewer sends your form back, what they asked for is shown in a highlighted box at the top of the form and in My Submissions.
- Needs Metatrash 0.95.21 for the ticks in the file tree (and, on the desktop, mtsync 0.95.21).
