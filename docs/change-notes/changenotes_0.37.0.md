# Slate Viewer 0.37.0

- **Spaces you manage for your organisation.** On metatrash.com, if you are an admin of an organisation, Settings > Spaces lists its spaces under its name, after your own. You open, share and change them just as you do your own spaces.
- **Move a space.** A space's Ownership page has Move: send one of your spaces to an organisation you manage, or bring it back to your own account. The address changes to the new owner's name, old links and connected apps still reach it, and its members stay. You'll be asked to confirm it's you.
- **Organisation tab.** Organisation admins see an Organisation tab in Settings with the organisation's spaces and people. There they add people, choose who is an admin, remove people and change the organisation's name. Members can leave from the same tab.
- Needs Metatrash 0.95.23 (with Company 0.96.32 for organisations). Older servers show Settings as before.
