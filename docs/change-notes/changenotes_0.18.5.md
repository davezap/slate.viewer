# Slate Viewer 0.18.5

## Changed

- **Links in a printout work anywhere.** When you print a document from a Metatrash space, its links to other files become full addresses on the website, such as `https://metatrash.com/spaces/owner/space/folder/file.md`. Click them in a PDF from anywhere; anyone who may read the space can open them.
- **No blank last page**, and no date or title at the top of printed pages.
- Lines that are only links, such as "Process Book home", are now always left off the printout.

## Needs

- Nothing new. Slate 0.19.5, included.
