# Slate Viewer 0.15.1

## New

- **See and switch the account.** Open Space now says which Metatrash account this computer is connected as. **Switch Account…** signs it in as another account: on the page that opens, choose **Sign out** next to the account name, then sign in as the other one. Every space then syncs as that account. Spaces only the old account can reach stop syncing until you switch back, and their files stay where they are.

## Fixed

- **No sign-in link in red.** If you cancel Connect More Spaces, or the sign-in stops without finishing, Open Space no longer shows the long sign-in link as an error.

## Needs

- Metatrash 0.49.1 and mtsync 0.49.1 to show the account name. With older versions, Switch Account still works but the name isn't shown.
