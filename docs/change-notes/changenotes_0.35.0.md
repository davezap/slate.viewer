# Slate Viewer 0.35.0

- **Forms fill themselves in.** A form's fields can start with an answer: your name, your email address, today's date, the time now, or any text the form's author chose. Change it if it's wrong.
- **Questions that appear when they're needed.** A form can ask a question only when an earlier answer calls for it, such as "What is damaged?" after you answer Yes to "Damaged?". Questions that aren't asked don't need answering and aren't sent.
- **Forms a step at a time.** A form can show its sections one after another: the next appears once you've answered the one before.
- **Photo questions.** Take a photo with your phone's camera, or choose pictures, right in the form. They're made smaller before sending and lose where they were taken; the filed copy shows them.
- The new Slate 0.22.0 editor, which reads these forms.
- Needs Metatrash 0.95.19 (and, on the desktop, mtsync 0.95.19 to send photos).
