# Slate Viewer 0.42.0

- **Folder Properties opens straight away** on the website, even in large spaces (0.41.2, never released alone).
- **Cleaner locks.** A locked setting shows a small padlock at the right of its section's title. Point at it to see who can't change it.
- **Automation is one list of actions.** Receive email is now an action next to the agent actions, and a folder can have several, each with its own address and senders. Add Action asks which kind you want, and you can drag actions to reorder them.
- **Deleting an action asks first.** The × became a bin, and nothing is deleted until you confirm.
- **New folders on the website.** Files has a New folder button and menu item, as the desktop app does.
- **Hidden files stay hidden.** On the website, AGENTS.md and files whose names start with a dot are hidden until you turn on the eye switch in Files (also on the Files menu you get by right-clicking). The desktop app has the same switch beside View › Show Hidden Files.
- **Search's Exact switch.** Best matches or exact text is now a small switch to the right of the search box.
