# Slate Viewer 0.36.0

- **Everything waiting for you, from all your spaces.** On metatrash.com the red number on the account icon now counts files to read, forms to review and forms sent back to you in every space you own or share, not just the one you have open. The account menu lists each space with something waiting; choose one to go there.
- **My Submissions covers all your spaces.** It opens from the account menu anywhere on the site and lists the forms you sent from every space, with the space shown on each one.
- **Use These Answers.** Pick a form you sent before and choose Use These Answers to open the form with those answers filled in, ready to change and send as a new copy. It's offered only while the form is the same as when you sent it. Photos aren't copied.
- **Not sent yet.** Forms you started and haven't sent are listed at the top of My Submissions. Choose Continue Filling In to pick up where you left off, or Discard Draft. Drafts are kept in the browser you typed them in.
- **Menus that match.** The account menu and the theme menu now look like Change Space: clear rows, a short line under each choice, and a tick on the one in use.
- **The documentation is back in reach.** metatrash.com now reopens the last place you were, so Change Space lists **Metatrash documentation** under Help.
- On the desktop app, My Submissions and the count still cover the open space.
- Needs Metatrash 0.95.22 for the all-spaces count and list; with an older server the website shows the open space's, as before.
