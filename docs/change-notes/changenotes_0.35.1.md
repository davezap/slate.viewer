# Slate Viewer 0.35.1

- **A red dot for forms waiting for you.** A new copy of a form sent to a folder you review gets a red dot, and so do the folders it's in, until someone approves it, declines it or asks for more. If a reviewer sends your form back for more information, the form gets a red dot for you until you fix your answers and send it again.
- **Clearer reviews.** After you approve, decline or ask for more, the review bar says what happened and who will see it, and the note box clears.
- Needs Metatrash 0.95.20 (and, on the desktop, mtsync 0.95.20 for the dots).
