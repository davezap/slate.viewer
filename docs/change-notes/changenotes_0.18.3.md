# Slate Viewer 0.18.3

## Changed

- **Printing flows better.** A `##` section that won't fit on what is left of a page starts on the next page, so a heading is no longer left at the bottom of a page with its text, table or diagram on the next. Table rows aren't split across pages.
- **Cleaner printouts.** The bar over a published document prints its version and reading status but not its buttons or prompts. Lines that are only links, such as "Process Book home", are left off.

## Needs

- Nothing new. Slate 0.19.3, included.
