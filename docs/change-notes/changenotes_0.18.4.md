# Slate Viewer 0.18.4

## Changed

- **Printouts are just the document.** No header, and no bar with the published version or reading status over the document. Each page has a footer with the document's name on the left and "Page 3 of 7" on the right.
- **Headings stay with their text.** A heading is never the last thing on a printed page; it moves to the next page with the table or text under it.

## Needs

- Nothing new. Slate 0.19.4, included.
