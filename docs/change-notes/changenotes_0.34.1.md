# Slate Viewer 0.34.1

- **Help on every Properties page.** Each page of a folder's **Properties** has a **?** button at the top right that opens its guide on metatrash.com: General, Security, Connections, Publishing, Automation and Settings File each have their own. The guides are under [Folder properties](https://metatrash.com/docs/guides/folder-properties/README.md).
- **Links that open parts of the app.** A link in a document can now open a page of Settings (`#settings/services/agents`), a folder's Properties (`#properties/<folder>/<page>`), show or hide a side panel (`#panel/files`, `#panel/outline/open`), or open the space picker, find bar and a few more (`#command/change-space`, `#command/find`). The button the link stands for flashes so you can find it next time. Guides use these to take you straight to the right place. These links only open or show things; they never save, delete or sign out.
- **Every document opens where you left it.** Switch to another document to look something up, then come back (by Back, a link or the Files list) and you're where you were, even after restarting. A document you haven't opened yet starts at its top. Turn this off, or forget the positions kept, under **Go** (on metatrash.com, in your account menu).
