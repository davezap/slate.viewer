# Slate Viewer 0.51.0

- **New folders and files appear straight away.** Press Enter in the name box and the new folder or file shows in Files at once, even in a big space. The box says it's making it and ignores a second Enter, so you no longer get "already exists" for something you can't see yet.
- **See what failed.** The line at the top of Files now says what went wrong, such as "2 files couldn't be converted", instead of just "2 failed". Click it for the list, with the reason for each, and click a file to open it. A closed folder with a problem inside shows a small red mark.
- **Sort out sync conflicts.** When a file changed in the space and on a computer at the same time, Metatrash Sync keeps both, and the bar at the top says "1 conflict". Click it to see each one, with what happened in plain words, and choose Compare and merge, Open copy, or Keep the space's version. This now works on the website too.
- **Your space stays open.** On the website, connecting Metatrash Sync from another tab, or a brief network hiccup, no longer closes the space you were reading.
