# Slate Viewer 0.17.0

## New

- **Quizzes.** A file you're asked to read can come with a quiz. Its bar then says **Take the Quiz** instead of I Have Read This. Answer the questions and choose **Send**: if you pass, the file counts as read and its dot goes. If you don't, the bar shows your score, and you can try again after a while (an hour unless the quiz says otherwise). **Fix My Answers** brings back your last answers with the ones that weren't right marked, and **Start Again** clears them. Written answers wait for a reviewer to mark them.
- **Every form is reviewed.** My Submissions now shows whether each form you sent is waiting for review, approved or declined, with the reviewer's note. For a quiz it shows your score and a ✓ or ✗ by each answer.
- **Review forms.** If you look after a review space, open a copy that was sent there and a bar lets you **Approve** or **Decline** it, with a note for the person who sent it. For a quiz, tick or cross each written answer, then choose **Reviewed**.
- **Use This as Answers.** To give a quiz its answers, take it yourself with the right answers, open your copy in the review space and choose **Use This as Answers**. It's saved as the quiz's answer key, which marks everyone's answers from then on. If the quiz changes, do it again for the new version.

## Changed

- **Forms are filled in as published.** In a folder that publishes versions, you now fill in and send the published version of a form, which is the one everyone sees. A draft of the form shows greyed out; choose **Show Published Version** to fill it in.

## Needs

- Metatrash 0.50.0 and mtsync 0.50.0 for quizzes, reviews and sending published forms. Slate 0.19.1, included.
