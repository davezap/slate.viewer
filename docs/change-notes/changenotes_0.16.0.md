# Slate Viewer 0.16.0

## Changed

- **Fill in forms straight away.** A form now opens ready to fill in when you view it in Preview. There's no Fill In button to press first. Your answers are saved on this computer as you type, so you can switch to Edit, close the app or come back tomorrow and carry on. Choose **Send** when you're done. After it's sent, the form is empty again in case you need to send another.
- **Edit shows the form itself.** In Edit and Source you change the form, with its `{{…}}` fields as text. **Fill In** on the bar takes you back to Preview.
- **Greyed-out fields when it can't be sent.** If the form isn't open from its Metatrash space, has unsaved changes, or you're looking at its published version, the fields show greyed out and the bar says why.
- **No front matter in Preview.** Preview no longer shows the `---` block at the top of a document (a form's title and where it's sent, for example). Edit and Source still show it.
