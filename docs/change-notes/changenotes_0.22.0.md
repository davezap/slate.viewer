# Slate Viewer 0.22.0

## New

- **Page footer.** Properties > Publishing has a **Page footer**: one line shown centred below a rule at the bottom of every page of the folder and its subfolders on metatrash.com, such as `:metatrash: is a ⚡[Zaptronics](https://zaptronics.co.nz/) product`. It takes links, **bold**, *italic* and `code`, and `:metatrash:` shows the Metatrash wordmark. A subfolder gets its parent's footer unless it sets its own, and **No footer here** turns it off. The footer is never added to the files. It needs Metatrash 0.88.0.

## Fixed

- On metatrash.com, opening the page you first came to after looking at other files puts its own address back in the address bar.
