# Slate Viewer 0.41.1

- **The email address straight away.** On the website, ticking Receive email in a folder's Properties creates the folder's address at once and shows it, ready to copy. Get a New Address does the same after asking whether to retire the old one.
- **Pictures show on the website.** Uploaded pictures no longer look greyed out in Files, and clicking one shows the picture instead of an empty note, with Copy Markdown to put it in a document (using its smaller preview copy when there is one). Other uploads, such as PDFs, open in a new tab.
