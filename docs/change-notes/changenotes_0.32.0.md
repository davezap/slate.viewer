# Slate Viewer 0.32.0

- **Ask for more on a form.** Reviewing a form's copy, choose **Ask for More** and write what is missing. The copy goes back to the person who sent it, with your note.
- **Fix it and send it again.** When that person opens the form, its bar shows the reviewer's note and **Fix My Answers**, which brings back what they sent. Sending it again files a fresh copy that replaces the old one, and the old copy says it was sent again. My Submissions shows "Needs more information" and "Sent again".
- Needs Metatrash 0.95.10 and, on the desktop, mtsync 0.95.10.
