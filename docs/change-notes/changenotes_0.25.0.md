# Slate Viewer 0.25.0

## New

- **Billing in Settings.** On metatrash.com, Settings has a Billing tab: your plan, when your free trial ends and when the first charge is, your storage allowance next to what your spaces use, and **Manage subscription**, which opens Paddle's billing page to change your card, read invoices or cancel. Without a plan it offers the free trial. It is in test mode for now: no real payments. Nothing changes in the desktop app. It needs Metatrash 0.91.0.
- **The pricing page knows you're subscribed.** Signed in with a plan, it shows Manage your plan instead of starting a second trial.
