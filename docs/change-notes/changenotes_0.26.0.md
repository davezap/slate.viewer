# Slate Viewer 0.26.0

## New

- **Add storage in Settings.** On metatrash.com, Settings → Billing → Storage shows how much of your storage your spaces use, with a bar, and lets you add or remove extra storage in 1 GB steps. **Check the price** shows what Paddle will charge, in your own currency with tax, before anything changes: during the free trial the extra storage is free until the trial ends. You can't go below what your spaces already use. It is in test mode for now: no real payments. Nothing changes in the desktop app. It needs Metatrash 0.92.0.
